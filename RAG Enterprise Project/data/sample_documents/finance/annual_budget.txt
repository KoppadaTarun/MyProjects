INTRODUCTION

This document defines the approved FY2026 operating budget for Northstar Systems, Inc.

The budget supports the company's enterprise software development, marketing activities, employee operations, infrastructure, and customer service functions.

All department heads are accountable for maintaining spending within their approved allocations.

Finance is responsible for monitoring expenditure, reviewing financial performance, and reporting budget variances.

1. TOTAL COMPANY BUDGET

The board has approved a total FY2026 operating budget of $3,000,000.

The approved departmental allocations are:

Engineering: $800,000
Marketing: $500,000
Human Resources: $200,000
Operations: $600,000
Finance and Corporate Administration: $300,000
Customer Success: $400,000

Total department allocations: $2,800,000

Central contingency reserve: $200,000

Total approved budget: $3,000,000

The contingency reserve is controlled by the CFO and cannot be accessed by individual departments without written CFO approval.

2. MARKETING DEPARTMENT BUDGET

The Marketing department receives an approved annual allocation of $500,000.

The allocation is distributed as follows:

Digital campaign expenditure: $120,000
Events and field marketing: $80,000
Content and creative production: $90,000
Marketing technology and analytics: $60,000
Research and agency services: $50,000
Uncommitted campaign capacity: $100,000

Total marketing allocation: $500,000

The digital campaign allocation includes search advertising, social media advertising, retargeting, and other digital distribution.

The approved digital channel allocation is:

Search advertising: $50,000
Social media advertising: $45,000
Retargeting and other digital distribution: $25,000

Marketing reports must distinguish approved allocations, actual expenditure, committed expenditure, and remaining available funds.

The Marketing Director may reallocate up to 10% between budget categories with written Finance notification, provided the total department allocation is not exceeded.

3. ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT BUDGET

Engineering receives $800,000 for product development, infrastructure, engineering tools, and technical reliability.

The allocation includes:

Cloud infrastructure and developer platforms: $360,000
Development tools and external specialists: $240,000
Security and reliability improvements: $120,000
Training and technical certification: $80,000

Total engineering allocation: $800,000

Cloud infrastructure commitments must be reviewed quarterly.

New recurring infrastructure commitments exceeding $25,000 annually require approval from the VP of Engineering and Finance Director before contract signature.

4. HUMAN RESOURCES BUDGET

Human Resources receives an annual allocation of $200,000.

The allocation is divided into:

Recruitment: $70,000
Employee learning and development: $45,000
Benefits administration and employee engagement: $55,000
HR systems and compliance: $30,000

Total HR allocation: $200,000

Eligible employees receive an annual learning allowance of $1,200 under the Employee Handbook.

The learning allowance must remain within the approved learning and development allocation.

5. OPERATIONS DEPARTMENT BUDGET

Operations receives an annual allocation of $600,000.

The allocation includes:

Facilities and workplace services: $250,000
Logistics and service providers: $180,000
Equipment and maintenance: $100,000
Operational contingency: $70,000

Total operations allocation: $600,000

Vendor selection and purchasing activities must follow OPS-POL-001, Vendor Management Policy, and OPS-POL-002, Procurement Guidelines.

6. FINANCIAL APPROVAL THRESHOLDS

Northstar uses the following spending approval matrix:

Up to $2,500:
Department Manager approval.

Above $2,500 and up to $10,000:
Department Director approval.

Above $10,000:
Finance Director approval before commitment.

Above $50,000:
Finance Director and CFO approval.

The approval threshold applies to the entire expected financial commitment, including applicable fees, taxes, and contract periods.

Splitting a purchase into multiple smaller invoices to avoid approval thresholds is strictly prohibited.

The department budget owner must verify fund availability before requesting approval.

7. MONTHLY FINANCIAL MONITORING

Department owners must submit monthly financial forecasts by the fifth business day of each month.

Finance distributes actual-versus-budget reports by the tenth business day.

A budget variance greater than 10% or $15,000, whichever threshold is reached first, requires a written explanation and corrective action plan.

Department owners must submit their explanations within five business days after receiving the variance report.

Reports must separately identify:
- Actual expenditure.
- Outstanding commitments.
- Forecast expenditure.
- Remaining budget.
- Budget variance.

8. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS

Employee reimbursements and business travel expenses must follow FIN-POL-002, Expense Reimbursement Policy.

Claims require itemized receipts, a business purpose, an appropriate cost center, and all required approvals.

Employees must submit claims within 30 calendar days after the expense date.

Managers cannot approve their own expenses.

9. GOVERNANCE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The CFO owns the annual budget.

Finance is responsible for:
- Maintaining financial records.
- Reviewing department expenditure.
- Monitoring budget compliance.
- Publishing monthly financial reports.
- Reviewing financial exceptions.

Department directors are responsible for managing their allocations.

Transfers between department budgets require CFO approval.

Finance may freeze discretionary spending when forecast revenue or cash conditions deteriorate materially.

All financial exceptions must be documented in the company's financial system.
