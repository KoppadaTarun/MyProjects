NORTHSTAR SYSTEMS, INC.
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Document ID: FIN-POL-002
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Finance
Applicable To: Employees and approved contractors

INTRODUCTION

This policy defines the procedures and requirements for reimbursing legitimate business expenses incurred by employees and approved contractors of Northstar Systems, Inc.

The policy covers travel, accommodation, meals, local transportation, software subscriptions, equipment, and other approved business purchases.

All expenses must comply with the FY2026 Annual Operating Budget FIN-BUD-001, Procurement Guidelines OPS-POL-002, and the Employee Handbook HR-POL-001.

1. GENERAL EXPENSE REQUIREMENTS

An expense is eligible for reimbursement only when it:
- Has a legitimate business purpose.
- Is reasonable in amount.
- Falls within an approved department budget.
- Has the necessary approval.
- Includes appropriate supporting documentation.

Employees should use company-issued payment cards whenever available.

Personal purchases, traffic fines, companion travel, and expenses unrelated to business activities are not reimbursable.

Alcohol expenses are not reimbursable unless a documented customer-event exception is approved in advance by the CFO and permitted by local law.

All expense claims must be submitted through the company expense portal within 30 calendar days after the expense date.

Each expense entry must contain:
- Transaction date.
- Merchant name.
- Amount and currency.
- Cost center.
- Business purpose.
- Supporting receipt.
- Attendee details when applicable.

Itemized receipts are mandatory for expenses of $25 or more.

For expenses below $25, a transaction record and written explanation are acceptable.

Employees may submit no more than two lost-receipt declarations per quarter. Each declaration requires manager approval.

2. FINANCIAL APPROVAL MATRIX

Northstar applies the following approval thresholds:

Expenses up to $2,500:
Department Manager approval.

Expenses above $2,500 and up to $10,000:
Department Director approval.

Expenses above $10,000:
Finance Director approval before the company commits to the expenditure.

Expenses above $50,000:
Finance Director and CFO approval.

Approval thresholds apply to the complete expected commitment, including fees, taxes, and contract periods where known.

Employees cannot approve their own expenses.

Manager approval confirms the business purpose and policy compliance but does not replace procurement, security, or legal reviews.

3. BUSINESS TRAVEL AND ACCOMMODATION

All business travel requires manager approval before booking.

International travel additionally requires Department Director approval and a documented business justification.

Employees should book economy-class air travel for flights shorter than six hours.

Premium economy may be approved by a Department Director for flights lasting six hours or longer.

Business-class travel requires CFO approval.

Hotel reimbursement guidelines are:

Standard-cost locations: Maximum $220 per night.
Designated high-cost cities: Maximum $300 per night.

Hotel limits exclude applicable taxes.

Any exception requires written Department Director approval before booking.

Employees should select refundable tickets only when travel schedules are uncertain or when the price difference is reasonable.

4. MEALS AND LOCAL TRANSPORTATION

Daily meal guidelines are:

Domestic business travel: $60 per day.
International business travel: $75 per day.

These limits include tips.

Customer-hosted meals exceeding the normal guideline require advance manager approval.

The expense claim must include the names of attendees and the business purpose.

Taxi and rideshare expenses are permitted when public transport is impractical, safety is a concern, or business equipment must be transported.

Premium vehicle categories require a documented accessibility or safety justification.

5. SOFTWARE AND EQUIPMENT PURCHASES

Software subscriptions, cloud services, and equipment cannot be purchased through personal expense claims when they require procurement, security, privacy, or legal review.

Software purchases require manager approval and Security review before use.

Equipment purchases must comply with Procurement Guidelines OPS-POL-002.

Company assets must be registered when required by the asset management process.

Personal devices and recurring personal subscriptions are not reimbursable without written business justification and approval.

6. CLAIM SUBMISSION AND PAYMENT

Employees submit claims through the expense portal.

Managers must review submitted claims within five business days.

Finance validates:
- Supporting receipts.
- Expense categorization.
- Budget availability.
- Required approvals.
- Compliance with company policy.

Approved reimbursements are normally processed in the next twice-monthly payment cycle.

Claims submitted after the 30-day deadline may be rejected unless the Finance Director approves an exception for documented circumstances.

7. AUDIT AND POLICY VIOLATIONS

Finance conducts monthly sample audits.

Finance may request original receipts, additional explanations, or confirmation of business purpose.

Duplicate claims, altered receipts, deliberate misclassification, and purchase splitting are prohibited.

Employees found violating this policy may be required to repay amounts and may face disciplinary action.

Suspected financial fraud must be escalated to the CFO and People Operations.

Expense records are retained for seven years or longer where local law requires.

8. EXAMPLES

Example 1:
A $2,000 business purchase requires Department Manager approval.

Example 2:
A $9,500 equipment purchase requires Department Director approval and procurement review.

Example 3:
A $12,000 vendor commitment requires Finance Director approval before signature.

Example 4:
A $62,000 commitment requires both Finance Director and CFO approval.

9. RESPONSIBILITIES

Employees are responsible for accurate claims and supporting documentation.

Managers validate business purpose and budget coding.

Department Directors approve expenses within their authority.

Finance validates financial controls and processes reimbursements.
