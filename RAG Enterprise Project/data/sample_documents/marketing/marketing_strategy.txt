Version: 1.2

INTRODUCTION

Northstar Systems, Inc. provides workflow automation and business analytics software to mid-market and enterprise customers.

The FY2026 marketing strategy focuses on increasing brand awareness, generating qualified sales opportunities, improving product adoption, and expanding the company's presence in North America and India.

The strategy is funded through the approved annual Marketing department budget of $500,000, documented in FIN-BUD-001.

1. BUSINESS AND MARKETING OBJECTIVES

The Marketing department has established the following measurable objectives for FY2026:

Generate 1,800 marketing-qualified leads (MQLs).

Influence $4.5 million in qualified sales pipeline.

Increase product-demo-to-opportunity conversion from 18% to 22% by December 31, 2026.

Increase website organic traffic by 30% compared with FY2025.

Improve brand recognition among enterprise technology buyers.

The department will prioritize organizations with 500 to 5,000 employees.

Primary target industries include:
- Financial services.
- Healthcare technology.
- Business services.
- Enterprise software.
- Logistics and supply chain management.

Marketing-qualified leads must meet the approved customer profile and demonstrate meaningful interest in Northstar's products.

2. TARGET AUDIENCES

The primary decision-makers are:

IT Directors:
Responsible for technology selection, platform reliability, and integration requirements.

Operations Leaders:
Interested in reducing manual processes and improving operational efficiency.

Transformation Executives:
Evaluate enterprise-wide automation opportunities.

Technical Evaluators:
Platform architects, security professionals, and engineering managers who assess technical feasibility.

Marketing campaigns must communicate measurable business benefits without making unsupported product claims.

Any technical statement about security, integrations, availability, or product performance must be reviewed by Engineering or Security before publication.

Customer names, logos, and testimonials require written permission from Customer Success and Legal.

3. APPROVED MARKETING BUDGET

The FY2026 Marketing department budget is $500,000.

The approved allocation is:

Digital campaign expenditure: $120,000.
Events and field marketing: $80,000.
Content and creative production: $90,000.
Marketing technology and analytics: $60,000.
Research and agency services: $50,000.
Uncommitted campaign capacity: $100,000.

Total approved allocation: $500,000.

The digital campaign allocation is divided into:

Search advertising: $50,000.
Social media advertising: $45,000.
Retargeting and other digital distribution: $25,000.

Total digital allocation: $120,000.

The Marketing Director may reallocate up to 10% between budget categories with written Finance notification, provided the department's total approved allocation is not exceeded.

Any transfer between departments requires CFO approval.

4. DIGITAL MARKETING STRATEGY

Northstar will use search advertising, professional social networks, targeted display campaigns, and retargeting.

Search advertising will focus on high-intent keywords related to workflow automation, enterprise integration, and business process optimization.

Social media advertising will primarily target IT Directors, Operations Managers, and enterprise decision-makers.

Retargeting will focus on website visitors who viewed product pages, pricing information, or technical documentation.

Marketing must track:
- Advertising expenditure.
- Cost per click.
- Cost per qualified lead.
- Conversion rate.
- Marketing-qualified leads.
- Sales-qualified leads.
- Pipeline contribution.

Campaign performance must be evaluated monthly.

5. CONTENT AND BRAND DEVELOPMENT

The content team will produce technical whitepapers, implementation guides, product demonstrations, customer case studies, and educational articles.

Engineering must review technical documentation before publication.

Customer Success must approve customer-related case studies.

Legal must review marketing material involving contractual claims, customer references, or regulated industries.

All published material must follow the Northstar brand guidelines.

6. EVENTS AND FIELD MARKETING

The company plans to participate in enterprise technology conferences and regional business events.

The approved FY2026 events and field marketing budget is $80,000.

Marketing must submit an event proposal containing:
- Event name and location.
- Estimated attendance.
- Target audience.
- Sponsorship cost.
- Expected qualified leads.
- Expected pipeline contribution.
- Post-event follow-up plan.

Department Director approval is required for event commitments above $2,500 and up to $10,000. Finance Director approval is required above $10,000.

7. PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT

Marketing performance is reviewed monthly.

The department publishes a campaign performance report containing:

Campaign expenditure.
Lead generation.
Cost per lead.
Website engagement.
Lead-to-opportunity conversion.
Pipeline contribution.
Budget utilization.

Finance receives expenditure information for reconciliation.

Sales and Customer Success provide feedback on lead quality and customer engagement.

8. DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

The Marketing Director owns the annual strategy and budget.

Campaign Managers manage individual campaigns and report their performance.

The Content Team maintains brand consistency and coordinates technical reviews.

Finance validates expenditure and budget availability.

Engineering reviews technical product claims.

Customer Success approves customer references.

Marketing reports must be submitted to Finance by the fifth business day of each month.
