INTRODUCTION

This report summarizes the performance and financial expenditure of Northstar Systems, Inc.'s digital marketing campaigns during the first three quarters of FY2026.

The report evaluates advertising expenditure, campaign effectiveness, lead generation, customer engagement, and budget utilization.

The approved annual Marketing department allocation is $500,000, as defined in FIN-BUD-001.

The approved digital campaign allocation is $120,000, as defined in MKT-STR-001.

1. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

The digital marketing program was designed to:

Increase qualified website traffic.

Generate marketing-qualified leads from enterprise customers.

Improve product awareness among IT and Operations decision-makers.

Generate qualified sales opportunities.

Support the annual marketing objective of influencing $4.5 million in sales pipeline.

The campaign primarily targeted organizations with 500 to 5,000 employees across financial services, healthcare technology, and business services.

2. DIGITAL ADVERTISING EXPENDITURE

The approved digital marketing budget was $120,000.

Actual expenditure during the reporting period was $120,000.

The expenditure breakdown is:

Search advertising: $50,000.
Social media advertising: $45,000.
Retargeting and other digital distribution: $25,000.

Total digital advertising expenditure: $120,000.

Budget utilization for digital campaigns was 100%.

Search advertising represented 41.67% of digital expenditure.

Social media advertising represented 37.50% of digital expenditure.

Retargeting and other digital distribution represented 20.83% of digital expenditure.

All three channel allocations were fully utilized during the reporting period.

3. SEARCH ADVERTISING PERFORMANCE

Search advertising expenditure was $50,000.

The search campaigns focused on high-intent enterprise software keywords.

Primary campaign categories included:
- Workflow automation.
- Enterprise process management.
- Business analytics platforms.
- Enterprise integration software.

Search advertising generated 1,100,000 impressions and 22,000 clicks.

The average cost per click was approximately $2.27.

The campaigns generated 520 marketing-qualified leads.

Search advertising contributed approximately $1.35 million in qualified sales pipeline.

The highest-performing search campaigns targeted enterprise workflow automation and integration-related keywords.

4. SOCIAL MEDIA ADVERTISING PERFORMANCE

Social media advertising expenditure was $45,000.

Campaigns primarily targeted IT Directors, Operations Managers, technology executives, and enterprise transformation professionals.

The campaigns generated:
- 1,500,000 impressions.
- 18,000 clicks.
- 360 marketing-qualified leads.
- Approximately $950,000 in qualified pipeline.

The average cost per click was $2.50.

Professional networking platforms generated the strongest engagement among enterprise decision-makers.

Social media campaigns were particularly effective for distributing technical whitepapers, product demonstrations, and customer success stories.

5. RETARGETING AND DIGITAL DISTRIBUTION

Retargeting and other digital distribution expenditure totaled $25,000.

The campaigns targeted visitors who had previously interacted with Northstar's website.

The program generated:
- 600,000 impressions.
- 10,000 clicks.
- 220 marketing-qualified leads.
- Approximately $700,000 in qualified pipeline.

Retargeting improved engagement among visitors who had previously viewed product information.

6. OVERALL CAMPAIGN RESULTS

Combined digital campaign performance:

Total expenditure: $120,000.
Total impressions: 3,200,000.
Total clicks: 50,000.
Total marketing-qualified leads: 1,100.
Total qualified pipeline contribution: $3,000,000.

Average cost per click: $2.40.

Average digital campaign cost per marketing-qualified lead: approximately $109.09.

Digital campaigns generated 1,100 MQLs against the annual company marketing target of 1,800 MQLs.

This represents approximately 61.11% of the annual lead-generation target.

7. MARKETING BUDGET UTILIZATION

The total approved FY2026 Marketing budget is $500,000.

Digital campaign expenditure was $120,000.

Other marketing categories have separate allocations:

Events and field marketing: $80,000.
Content and creative production: $90,000.
Marketing technology and analytics: $60,000.
Research and agency services: $50,000.
Uncommitted campaign capacity: $100,000.

At the reporting date, the digital campaign allocation had been fully spent.

The remaining Marketing department budget allocation was $380,000.

This figure represents the difference between the total approved department budget and the reported digital campaign expenditure. It does not represent a verified cash balance after other commitments or expenditure in the remaining categories.

8. PERFORMANCE OBSERVATIONS

Search advertising produced the highest qualified pipeline contribution at $1.35 million.

Social media advertising generated 360 MQLs and supported enterprise brand awareness.

Retargeting generated 220 MQLs and improved engagement with returning website visitors.

The combined digital campaign expenditure was consistent with the approved $120,000 digital allocation.

9. RECOMMENDATIONS

Marketing should prioritize high-intent search campaigns in the next planning cycle.

The team should improve conversion tracking between marketing-qualified leads and sales-qualified opportunities.

Retargeting audiences should be segmented based on product-page engagement.

Finance and Marketing should reconcile all remaining budget categories before approving additional campaigns.

10. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Marketing Director owns campaign performance.

Campaign Managers maintain campaign records and expenditure details.

Marketing Analytics prepares monthly reports.

Finance validates expenditure and budget utilization.

Sales reviews lead quality and pipeline conversion.

This report is maintained by the Marketing Analytics Team.
