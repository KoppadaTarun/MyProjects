INTRODUCTION

This Employee Handbook defines the employment policies, workplace expectations, employee benefits, and professional responsibilities at Northstar Systems, Inc. Northstar Systems is a business-to-business software company headquartered in Austin, Texas, with offices and employees in Hyderabad, India, and other remote locations.

This handbook applies to all regular full-time employees unless a particular section or local employment addendum specifies otherwise. Applicable employment laws and signed employment agreements take precedence whenever they provide greater employee protections.

1. EMPLOYMENT AND WORKING ARRANGEMENTS

Northstar Systems follows a standard Monday-to-Friday working schedule consisting of 40 working hours per week.

The normal collaboration window is between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. Employees may organize their remaining working hours flexibly, subject to manager approval and project requirements.

Employees can work from the office, follow a hybrid arrangement, or work remotely depending on their role and approved working arrangement.

New employees complete a 90-calendar-day onboarding period. During this period, managers must schedule:
- Role-specific orientation.
- Information security training.
- Introduction to company policies.
- A 30-day performance discussion.
- A 60-day progress review.
- A 90-day onboarding assessment.

Managers are responsible for ensuring that new employees receive the necessary system access, equipment, and training.

2. WORKPLACE CONDUCT AND PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR

Employees must maintain professional behavior when interacting with colleagues, customers, vendors, and business partners.

Northstar strictly prohibits harassment, discrimination, retaliation, intimidation, and inappropriate workplace conduct.

Employees can report workplace concerns to their direct manager, People Operations, or the confidential ethics reporting channel.

People Operations must acknowledge reports within two business days whenever contact information is available. An initial investigation assessment should normally be completed within ten business days.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest before participating in relevant business decisions.

Examples include:
- Evaluating a supplier owned by a family member.
- Receiving personal gifts from a business partner.
- Working for a competing organization.
- Participating in recruitment decisions involving relatives.

Conflict disclosures must be submitted to the employee's manager and People Operations.

3. COMPENSATION AND EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

Northstar processes payroll twice monthly:
- First payment: 15th calendar day.
- Second payment: Final calendar day of the month.

If a payment date falls on a bank holiday, payment is processed on the preceding business day.

Eligible full-time employees may enroll in medical, dental, vision, life insurance, and retirement benefit programs.

New employees generally have 30 calendar days from their employment start date to complete their initial benefit selections.

The company also provides an annual professional learning allowance of $1,200 per eligible employee.

The learning allowance can be used for:
- Technical certifications.
- Professional courses.
- Industry conferences.
- Role-related training programs.

Manager approval is mandatory before purchasing any training or certification.

Employees must provide proof of course completion or conference attendance within 30 days after the activity.

4. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

Managers must conduct at least two formal performance discussions each calendar year.

The standard review periods are June and December.

Performance evaluations consider:
- Quality of work.
- Achievement of assigned objectives.
- Technical and professional development.
- Collaboration with other departments.
- Customer impact.
- Compliance with company policies.

Employees should maintain their performance objectives in the HR portal.

Promotion decisions are based on sustained performance, demonstrated capabilities, business requirements, and readiness for additional responsibilities.

Length of employment alone does not guarantee promotion.

5. INFORMATION SECURITY AND COMPANY PROPERTY

All employees must protect confidential company information and customer data.

Company-issued devices must have:
- Full-disk encryption.
- Approved endpoint protection.
- Automatic screen locking.
- Updated operating systems.
- Approved software installations.

Employees must never share account credentials or store confidential company information in personal cloud storage.

Suspected credential exposure, device loss, or accidental information disclosure must be reported to Security Operations immediately and no later than one hour after discovery.

Employees leaving the company must return all company equipment, access cards, and other company property by their final working day.

6. TRAVEL AND BUSINESS EXPENSES

Business travel requires manager approval before booking.

Employees should select economical travel options that satisfy business requirements.

All travel and reimbursement claims must comply with the Expense Reimbursement Policy FIN-POL-002.

Employees must:
- Provide itemized receipts.
- Document the business purpose.
- Use the appropriate department cost center.
- Submit claims within 30 calendar days after the expense date.
- Obtain the required approvals.

Employees cannot approve their own expense claims.

7. LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE

Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual paid leave per calendar year.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of five unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. These carried-forward days must be used by March 31.

Employees also receive up to ten paid sick leave days annually, subject to applicable local employment laws.

Detailed leave entitlements, request procedures, approval responsibilities, and cancellation rules are documented in HR-POL-002, the Leave and Attendance Policy.

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal.

8. EMPLOYEE AND MANAGEMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Employees are responsible for:
- Following company policies.
- Protecting confidential information.
- Completing mandatory training.
- Recording attendance accurately.
- Submitting accurate expense claims.
- Maintaining professional conduct.

Managers are responsible for:
- Supporting employee development.
- Applying policies consistently.
- Reviewing leave requests.
- Maintaining adequate team coverage.
- Escalating policy exceptions.

People Operations owns this handbook and is responsible for publishing policy revisions.
