INTRODUCTION

This policy establishes the procedures for requesting, approving, recording, and managing employee leave at Northstar Systems, Inc.

The policy applies to regular employees working in the United States and India, subject to country-specific employment laws and local policy addenda.

The purpose of this policy is to support employee well-being while ensuring adequate business continuity and team availability.

1. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT

Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave for every completed calendar year.

Employees joining during a calendar year receive a prorated leave balance based on completed months of service.

Part-time employees receive leave proportional to their contracted working hours.

Annual leave is calculated using working days. Weekends and designated public holidays are excluded.

Employees can carry forward a maximum of five unused annual leave days into the following calendar year.

Carried-forward leave must be utilized by March 31.

Any remaining carried-forward balance expires after March 31 unless applicable employment legislation prohibits such expiry.

For example, an employee having seven unused annual leave days on December 31 can carry forward only five days. The remaining two days cannot be transferred unless local law requires a different treatment.

Annual leave is not automatically converted into cash during employment. Final settlement is handled according to applicable local employment laws.

2. LEAVE REQUEST AND APPROVAL PROCEDURE

Employees must submit annual leave requests through the official HR portal.

The following advance-notice requirements apply:

- One or two consecutive working days: At least three working days' notice.
- Three or more consecutive working days: At least ten working days' notice.
- Five or more consecutive working days: A documented team coverage plan is mandatory.

Each request must contain:
- Leave category.
- Start date.
- End date.
- Number of working days.
- Handover contact, when applicable.
- Additional comments, if necessary.

The employee's direct manager is responsible for reviewing and approving requests.

Managers should normally respond within three working days.

A leave request is considered approved only when its status is marked Approved in the HR portal.

A verbal conversation or informal message from a manager does not replace formal approval.

Managers should consider:
- Existing team commitments.
- Customer support requirements.
- Critical project releases.
- Previously approved employee absences.
- Available team coverage.

When multiple employees request overlapping leave dates, managers must evaluate requests fairly.

3. SICK LEAVE

Eligible employees may use up to ten paid sick leave days per calendar year, subject to statutory entitlements and local employment addenda.

Employees should notify their direct manager before their scheduled working hours whenever possible.

If an employee is absent for more than two consecutive working days, People Operations may request medical documentation where permitted by applicable law.

Medical information must be submitted directly to People Operations rather than shared in public team channels.

Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the next calendar year and is not converted into cash unless required by local law.

Managers must protect employee medical privacy.

4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS AND OTHER LEAVE

Northstar observes public holidays applicable to each employee's registered work location.

People Operations publishes the following year's holiday calendar before December 1.

Employees required to work on a designated public holiday should coordinate compensatory time or other applicable arrangements with their manager.

Parental leave, bereavement leave, jury duty, civic duty, and other statutory leave are administered according to applicable local legislation and company benefit plans.

Employees should contact People Operations for country-specific entitlements.

5. LEAVE CANCELLATION AND EMERGENCY ABSENCES

Employees must cancel or modify previously approved leave through the HR portal when their plans change.

Managers may request reconsideration of approved leave only when an unforeseen and significant business disruption occurs.

Any company-requested cancellation must be discussed with the employee and People Operations.

Reasonable unrecoverable travel expenses may be reviewed for reimbursement.

For unexpected emergencies, employees or their representatives should notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

The manager must coordinate with People Operations to determine the correct absence category.

Employees must not be penalized for delays caused by genuine emergencies.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES

Employees are responsible for submitting accurate leave requests, providing appropriate notice, and completing necessary handovers.

Managers must:
- Review leave requests promptly.
- Maintain sufficient operational coverage.
- Avoid discouraging legitimate leave.
- Apply policies consistently.
- Escalate exceptional cases.

People Operations is responsible for maintaining leave balances, interpreting the policy, and resolving disputes.

Employees should verify their leave balances in the HR portal before making non-refundable travel bookings.

7. PRACTICAL EXAMPLES

Example 1:
An employee has seven unused annual leave days on December 31. Only five days can be carried forward.

Example 2:
An employee plans to take Monday through Friday off. The request should be submitted at least ten working days in advance, and a handover contact must be identified.

Example 3:
An employee requires one working day off for a personal appointment. The employee should normally submit the request three working days beforehand.

8. POLICY EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions require written approval from the Head of People Operations.

Managers cannot independently increase annual leave entitlements, waive statutory requirements, or promise cash conversion.

This policy is reviewed annually. The latest approved version in the company policy library supersedes downloaded copies.
